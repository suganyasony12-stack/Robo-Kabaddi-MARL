# env_tests — checks that run without PyBullet

These tests replace PyBullet and gymnasium with small recording stand-ins in
`mock/`, so the environment's control flow can be checked on any machine.

**They check code, not physics.** The mock integrator is frictionless and
produces no contacts. Physical behaviour must be measured with real PyBullet —
see `../exp_L_locomotion_fix.py`.

| Script | What it proves |
|---|---|
| `compare_legacy.py` | `locomotion="legacy"` issues exactly the same PyBullet calls, in the same order with the same arguments, and returns exactly the same observations as the code behind the submitted results (`envs/kabaddi_env_legacy_backup.py`). 36 cases: 1/3/5 defenders × static/reactive/wall × 4 seeds, including runs that drive across the midline so the reactive controller reads positions. |
| `check_planar.py` | The corrected model's wiring: 1/240 s physics, 12 sub-steps per control step, command re-applied at every sub-step, robots held upright every sub-step, 1.8 kg, zero floor friction and damping, contact peak across sub-steps reaching both the contact flag and the observation, runtime patches of `_reactive` still taking effect, the `ROBO_KABADDI_LOCOMOTION` override, and unchanged observation width (so trained-policy input shapes are unaffected). |
| `check_physical_defenders.py` | `defender_type="physical"` follows the physical defenders' controller rule by rule: still until the raider is in the defenders' half, 0.30 m/s (the raider's maximum), eight directions, 5 Hz pursuit with the command held, soft line 0.30 m before contact and released after, 0.15 m midline guard and 0.30 m wall margins at every step, `diagonal_speed_ratio`, never entering the raider half or exceeding 0.30 m/s over driven raids, unchanged observation width. Also `start_layout`: legacy starts identical to the submitted code for 1-7 defenders, and `back_row` starts 0.13-0.31 m from the back wall, in order, without overlap. |
| `check_outcome_rules.py` | `outcome_rules="section_2_3"`: a raider stranded past the midline at timeout is a Tackle (−5) with or without contact, where `legacy` says Empty (−2); the raid timer starts at the midline crossing; a raider that never crosses ends Empty at 20 s; touch-and-return and come-home-without-contact are unchanged; a crossing on the last approach step starts the raid; observation unchanged; default is `legacy`. |
| `check_tackle_rules.py` | `outcome_rules="physical"`: a raider within 5 cm for more than 7 s in the defenders' half is a Tackle (step 141), before or after contact; creeping faster than 5 cm per 7 s is not; the window clears outside the defenders' half; `section_2_3` and `legacy` unchanged. |
| `check_contact_rules.py` | `contact_min_depth`, `contact_confirm_steps` (cumulative, latched) and `contact_model="camera"` with `contact_dist`: shallow contact ignored, two separate steps count, camera distance decides contact, the observation shows raw proximity (1.0/0.0, left) while the game uses the rules, no Touch from an uncounted contact; defaults unchanged. |
| `check_robot_shape.py` | `robot_shape="box"` builds 0.17 × 0.17 × 0.315 m box robots (collision and visual) with the same mass, spawn positions and observation as the cylinder; runs under both locomotion models; default is the cylinder. |
| `check_force_drive.py` | `locomotion="planar_force"`: one capped drive force per robot per sub-step (1.7 N driving, 5.1 N resisting), velocity never set to the command, free motion reaching 0.30 m/s in 0.25-0.35 s, gain set from `push_yield`, other modes apply no forces. |
| `check_hardware_raider.py` | `hardware_raider.py`: its command mapping equals `smorphi_controller.action_to_cmd` on a 81 x 81 action grid; with all switches off it equals the Experiment O raider; start band, 5 Hz hold, retreat at the next decision, retreat law, edge escape, diagonal ratio and robot-frame rotation. |
| `check_gate_defenders.py` | `defender_type="gate"` (v2): still while the raider is home; starts when it crosses; slots -0.19 / 0 / +0.19 m by left-to-right order, 0.25 m behind; step sideways out to 0.50 m, pass in the outer lane, close diagonally once level; never within touching range while passing; no step toward the midline inside 0.40 m before contact; after contact hold slot depth; wall and guard escapes; never in the raider half; ≤ 0.30 m/s; 5 Hz hold; 1-7 defenders. Section 9: `gate_style="keepout"` (default): early diagonal, backing away, no chosen move inside 0.5 m from ≥ 0.56 m, pressing after contact, closest ≥ 0.45 m in driven raids. Section 10: `gate_style="v7"`: goes back near the midline (and not outside the band), keeps going back until 0.55 m deeper than the raider or the rear margin (wall-edge fix), not after contact, moves when cornered (v6 stops there), never stops in 300 layouts, keep-out kept, still while the raider is home. Section 11: `gate_style="v8"`: holds the back row and shadows sideways (never toward the midline, never stopping) until the raider is 1.0 m in, then closes and stays closing; no step toward the midline inside 0.60 m before contact; pressing after contact; keep-out in 300 layouts; driven raids ≥ 0.45 m while holding, ≥ 0.40 m when driven to 1.2 m. Section 12: `gate_style="v9"`: side defenders come forward to 1.0 m and 0.8 m to each side, the centre one stays on the back row; shadowing; closing starts when the raider is 1.0 m in, side defenders head for its midline side, the centre one stays deep until contact; no step toward the midline inside 0.60 m; never stops and keep-out in 300 layouts; in driven raids (in to 1.45 m, then home) a defender is on the raider's midline side in every raid, 1 vs 3 and 1 vs 2; side defenders ≥ 0.40 m. Section 13: `gate_style="v10"`: holding as v9; two blockers once closing (the best placed); after contact a blocker moves straight sideways or diagonally, whichever covers the raider's way home best; drives at the raider within 0.35 m on its midline side; never stops, keep-out and the 0.60 m line in 300 layouts. Section 14: `gate_style="v11"`: wall margin 0.12 m (whole robot inside the line) instead of 0.30 m, only for v11; a blocker follows the raider into the side strip, v10 cannot. |
| `check_raider_command.py` | `raider_command="hardware"` equals the robots' `action_to_cmd` on 6,561 actions (turning ignored, diagonal ≤ 0.30 m/s); `action_repeat=4` = one 5 Hz decision (48 sub-steps, summed reward, returns when the raid ends); `raider_start="near_midline"` band with unchanged defender starts; `CORRECTED_CONFIG` builds and runs; defaults unchanged. |
| `check_court_rules.py` | `court_walls=False` builds no walls; raider centre over any line, at any time, is a Tackle under `outcome_rules="physical"` (not under the other rules); defenders stay inside without walls; `CORRECTED_CONFIG` holds the author's A2/A4/A5 choices and runs. |
| `check_learned_defenders.py` | `defender_type="learned"` and `defender_fouls` (C3, author's rules 27 Oct): the 9 actions are the robots' defender commands (stop and 8 directions at 0.30 m/s, `diagonal_speed_ratio` on diagonals), held for one env.step (4 control steps), no boundary escape; a defender centre over the midline, a side line or the end line, before or after the raider crosses, ends the raid as `foul` with one point per defender over (team +n / -n, raider n x R_TOUCH); just inside is no foul; off by default and never for the gate; team rewards: touch +1/-1, raider over a line (tackle) -1/+1, Empty -0.3 for both; defender observations (N x 15+2(N-1), egocentric, teammates nearest first) and the critic state (4+4N+3) for 1/2/3/7 defenders; raider observation unchanged; replaying the gate's commands as actions gives the same raids. The same replay with real PyBullet: `../check_learned_equivalence.py`. Section 7, `raider_edge_escape`: off by default; within 0.30 m of a side line or either end line the raider's command becomes the robots' escape at 0.30 m/s (diagonal in a corner, `diagonal_speed_ratio`), its own command 0.31 m away or with the option off; not a wall: a raider pushed over a line is still tackled. Section 8, `held_needs_touch` (author, 28 Oct): off by default (a still raider held after 4 s as before); on, the 4 s clock runs only while a defender touches the raider and restarts when the touch is lost; with no touch a still raider is tackled at the 30 s timeout. |

Re-run all thirteen after any edit to `envs/kabaddi_env.py`. If `compare_legacy.py`
ever reports a difference, the submitted results are no longer reproducible
and the edit must be fixed before anything else.

Do not point these at the results folder: the mock produces meaningless
numbers, and they must never be mistaken for measurements.
